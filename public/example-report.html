Add Product & Offer Schema
🔍 Diagnosis
No JSON-LD schema markup was identified in the analyzed content, missing critical structured data for vehicles, pricing, and availability.
⚠️ Why it's critical
Search engines and AI crawlers rely on schema to understand product details, pricing, and inventory—this directly impacts rich snippets and AI model training.
✅ What to do
Implement Product, AggregateOffer, and Organization schema across vehicle listing pages and homepage to signal structured vehicle data.
🛠️ How to do it
Add JSON-LD blocks in the <head> for each vehicle model with properties like name, description, image, price, and availability. Use AggregateOffer to show price ranges and dealer availability across regions.
💡 Concrete example
A RAV4 product page should include {"@type": "Product", "name": "2026 RAV4", "offers": {"@type": "AggregateOffer", "priceCurrency": "USD", "price": "28500"}}.
📈 Expected impact
Improved visibility in search results, better AI understanding of inventory, and potential for rich snippets showing vehicle specs and pricing.
🎯 Expert tip
Prioritize schema on your top 10 vehicle models first, then roll out systematically—this signals to AI systems that your site is a trusted automotive authority.
Technical guide

Implement at minimum these JSON-LD schemas: Organization (name, logo, contact, social links), FAQPage (your FAQs), Article or BlogPosting (for each editorial content). For e-commerce, add Product with price, availability, aggregate reviews (AggregateRating). Validate with the Google Rich Results Test. Pages with structured Schema are cited 3-5x more often by AI (Otterly.AI, 2026).

Documented real case

A website increased its AI visibility by 340% in 6 months through content restructuring and schema implementation. AI traffic went from 127 to 2,340 monthly sessions. (Stackmatix, 2026)
